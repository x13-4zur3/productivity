# What's new

## Productivity 1.1.0

**Upcoming Windows and Linux release.** See [Build from source](build.md) to try 1.1.0 before installers are published.

- **Planning** adds Month and Linear calendar views, linked journal tasks, and a weekly planner for rescheduling work.
- **Search** opens alongside your work. Use **Ctrl+F** to open or close it, and filter results by Journal, Tasks, or Events.
- **Focus and appearance** add custom 1–180 minute timers, a Pink theme, and more configurable shortcuts.
- **Desktop controls** include a themed Windows title bar and subtle transitions that respect reduced-motion settings.
- **Undo** gives you 30 seconds to reverse a deletion from the in-app notification.

Local databases upgrade when 1.1.0 starts. Signed-in users receive the new tools when their account server is updated. [Account hosting guide](hosting.md)
